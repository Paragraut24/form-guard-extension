🛡️ PhishGuard Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 13, 2026

TL;DR: PhishGuard protects your privacy. We store minimal data locally on your device, encrypt sensitive information, and never sell your data.

1. Information We Collect

PhishGuard collects the following information to provide phishing protection:

1.1 Stored Locally on Your Device

  • URL Analysis History: Up to 100 most recent scanned URLs with risk scores (stored locally, not synced)
  • Scan Statistics: Total scans, blocked sites, warnings shown (local only)
  • Whitelist/Blacklist: Domains you manually trust or block (synced across your Chrome browsers)
  • Settings: Your preferences (API key, notification settings, theme)

1.2 Data We DO NOT Collect

  • ❌ We do NOT collect your browsing history
  • ❌ We do NOT track your identity or profile you
  • ❌ We do NOT share data with advertisers
  • ❌ We do NOT have access to your passwords or form data

2. How We Use Your Information

  • URL Analysis: Analyzed URLs are checked against threat databases to protect you from phishing
  • Scan History: Kept locally to show you your protection activity
  • Statistics: Used to display your dashboard metrics
  • Whitelist/Blacklist: Applied to skip trusted sites and block known threats

3. Third-Party Services

PhishGuard may communicate with external services to enhance protection:

3.1 VirusTotal (Optional)

  • What's sent: URLs you visit (only if you provide an API key)
  • Purpose: Check URLs against 70+ security engines
  • Privacy: Read VirusTotal's Privacy Policy
  • Your control: This is opt-in — works without an API key using local detection only

3.2 OpenPhish

  • What's sent: No personal data sent
  • Purpose: Fetch live phishing threat feed for Global Threats dashboard
  • Privacy: Read OpenPhish Privacy Policy

4. Data Security

  • API Keys: Encrypted using AES-256-GCM before storage
  • Local Storage: Data stored in Chrome's secure storage (not accessible to websites)
  • No Cloud Storage: Your data never leaves your device except for API calls you initiate
  • HTTPS Only: All external API calls use encrypted connections

5. Data Retention

  • Scan History: Last 100 URLs (automatically rotated)
  • Statistics: Kept until you clear them or uninstall the extension
  • Cache: URL scan results cached for 7 days
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the extension removes all local data immediately

6. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • View: See your scan history and statistics in the popup
  • Delete: Clear history anytime via "Clear History" button
  • Export: Use browser DevTools to access chrome.storage if needed
  • Opt-out: Simply don't provide an API key to avoid external API calls

7. Children's Privacy

PhishGuard does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The extension is intended for general audiences.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by updating the "Last Updated" date. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Compliance

  • GDPR: We comply with EU data protection regulations
  • CCPA: We comply with California privacy laws
  • Chrome Web Store: Compliant with Google's User Data Policy
